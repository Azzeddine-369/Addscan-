Additive Details

E330
Citric Acid
Acidity Regulator / Antioxidant
Generally Recognized as Safe
Widely used in food, with no major health concerns at normal levels.
🌱 Vegan Friendly
🌾 No Major Allergen
🌐 Natural Origin
🧪
Overview & Role in Food
Acidulant and pH regulator. Helps to maintain acidity, enhance flavour and act as a preservative.
🌱
Origin / Source
Naturally found in citrus fruits. Can also be produced synthetically via Aspergillus niger fermentation.
🍽️
Common Foods Where It Appears
Soft drinks, candies, jams, baked goods, dairy products, canned vegetables.
🛡️
Regulatory & Safety Notes
Approved by EFSA, FDA and Codex Alimentarius. Generally recognized as safe at typical use levels.
